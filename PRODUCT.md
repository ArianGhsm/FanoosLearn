# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Candidates preparing for Iranian dental specialty examinations (inferred from the repository). They use Persian, on mobile and desktop, across repeated study sessions.

## Product Purpose

Make it possible to find, practise and review past examination questions and follow study progress.

## Positioning

Questions are connected to examination years and reference editions, with source evidence where verified. The bank is the authority for question facts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing PHP site includes question bank, exam runner, review, plans, progress, account and commerce. Preserve the existing routes, entitlements, accessible interactions, frozen attempts and factual qualifications. The website is RTL and supports light and dark themes. Bots and production data are outside the redesign.

## Brand Commitments

Fanoos / فانوس, the lantern mark, the existing Persian typeface and brand colours are established in the repository. The owner requested an extensive website redesign informed by the design and motion resources in the task.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing server-rendered interfaces and actual product content; do not invent results, testimonials or reference coverage.
