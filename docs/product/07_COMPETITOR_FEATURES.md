# 07 — What MedoFast and Parseh offer, and where FANOOS stands

Reviewed on 2026-10-03 from public pages only (no account was created on
either site):

- **MedoFast** (medofast.ir) — the free bank of national medical/dental exams
  the owner named as the model. Its residency bank is medical; its dental
  coverage is the basic-sciences exam. What matters here is the product
  surface, which is the same for every bank.
- **Parseh** (dparseh.ir, پیشگامان پارسه) — the dental residency institute.
  Its question bank ships inside paid packages (Windows/Android "سامانه جامع
  پارسه"), next to printed books, summary modules and comprehensive mock
  exams. Its site only answers reliably from inside Iran.

Each row is one option a student can see. **M** = MedoFast, **P** = Parseh.
Status: ✅ on fanooslearn.ir · 🟡 partly · ❌ not yet. The target is ≥ 80 %
of the counted rows at ✅.
